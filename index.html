Welcome! I am a PhD student in Economics at the Tepper School of Business, Carnegie Mellon University.

My research lies at the intersection of industrial organization and urban economics. I combine structural modeling with large-scale data to study the spatial distribution of retail firms and consumers' travel patterns, with applications to remote work and curbside parking.
